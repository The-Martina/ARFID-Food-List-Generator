About This Tool

This application was designed specifically for people with Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder (ARFID) to help them identify, organize, and document their safe foods.

Living with ARFID can make it challenging to maintain a varied diet. This tool allows you to:

Create a List

Select foods from different categories that you feel comfortable eating.

Add Details

Include specific brands you prefer and personal notes about each food.

Download & Share

Export your list as a PDF or image to share with healthcare providers or keep for reference.
